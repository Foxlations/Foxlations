Foxlations v1.1.0

A new Discover feed, one-tap source setup, faster source lists, more languages,
and a batch of fixes.

Highlights
- Discover new series — a new Discover tab suggests manga, anime, and light novels
  one at a time. Tap Next for another, add it to your library when one of your
  sources carries it, or save it for later. Filter by genre, and link a tracker
  for picks based on what you have already read.
- One-tap source install — on a source site, its "add to app" button now opens
  Foxlations and adds the repo for you; just confirm.
- Faster, lighter source lists — large source repos load from a compact index
  that's a fraction of the size, so adding and refreshing them is quicker on
  mobile data.
- Filter Browse by language and repository — the filter is now a pop-up with
  Language and Repository tabs; pick any combination to narrow a long list.
- Nine languages — the whole app is translated into English, Spanish, Portuguese,
  French, German, Italian, Japanese, Chinese, and Arabic (Settings > Language),
  and more can now be added by the community.
- Right-to-left layouts — Arabic and other right-to-left languages mirror the
  interface correctly.
- iOS, Android, and Windows.

Also in this release
- Manga, anime, and novels each keep their own progress, so Continue lands in the
  right place for every type.
- Fixes: manga reliably continues from your saved page; moving titles in and out
  of the vault works again and keeps your progress; and you can no longer install
  the same source twice.

Full change list is in the app: More > About > What's New.
